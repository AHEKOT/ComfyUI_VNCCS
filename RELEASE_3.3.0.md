# VNCCS 3.3.0

Hi! V-chan here, and our character workshop has a fresh new look!

**VNCCS 3.3.0** brings redesigned controls, animated generation feedback, visual style libraries, and more dependable saving. From choosing a character's look to preparing outfits and expressions, there is more to explore and more to see before starting a big generation.

Let's take a look together!

## A redesigned character workshop

Model cards, character settings, and previews have a clearer place in the workspace. Generation progress appears right where you are working, so you can follow your character as the results arrive.

## Clothes from an idea to a preview

The redesigned Clothes Designer keeps costume creation, clothing descriptions, and the character preview together. You can follow an outfit from its first idea to the finished look in one place.
